# Changelog

Short notes per version. The app shows the new ones once after an update, and
Settings > About > What's new shows them again. The other files in this folder hold the
same notes in the other interface languages.

## 1.1.5

- **Updates install automatically.** When a new version is out, the app installs it once Fortnite is closed. Turn it off below or in Settings > About.
- **16 more languages.** Every language Fortnite has: German, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Turkish, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) and Arabic. The app starts in the language of Windows; Settings > General switches it.
- **New Settings.** Categories on the left, a switch for each option, and every change is saved as you make it.
- **Six color themes.** Midnight stays the default; Graphite, Deep sea, Amethyst, OLED (true black) and the light Parchment are in Settings > General.
- **Your chests in Discord.** While Fortnite runs, your Discord profile shows your total, today's chests and the match timer, with a button for friends to get the app. Settings > OBS and Discord picks what to show or turns it off.
- **About.** Version, updates, help and the open-source licenses in one place. Copy app info puts what a bug report needs on the clipboard.
- **Change your answer about an account.** Settings > Counting lists the other accounts that played on this PC. One marked as yours or not by mistake can be switched back, and its base and prizes are kept.
- **Star on GitHub** link in the menu.

## 1.1.4

- **Russian interface.** Pick the language at first start or in Settings > Language.
- **Several accounts on one PC.** Each Epic account gets its own total, and the menu switches between them. When replays of a new account show up, the app asks whether it is yours. A new name in Epic is picked up by itself, so there is no name to type anymore.
- **Match clock on the overlay.** Next to IN MATCH, how long the match has run since it loaded.
- **The overlay hides behind other windows.** Alt+Tab out of Fortnite and it goes; come back and it is there. Settings > Overlay turns this off.
- **Goes back to waiting after the game.** When the app starts with Fortnite, it returns to waiting a minute after Fortnite closes.
- **Dug-up chests count.** Buried chests that show up already opened when dug up are counted now. Your matches are counted again once after the update.
- Fixed: a match removed from the list stays removed after a restart, updates download on slow connections, and checkboxes in Settings apply right away.

## 1.1.3

- **Leaderboard.** Everyone who runs the app with Online on, by chests, best match and matches, for all time, this week or today. Click a column to sort. Settings > Online shows you as Anonymous instead of your name.
- **Starts with Fortnite.** After you sign in to Windows the app waits quietly and opens in the tray once Fortnite starts. Settings > Windows switches back to starting with Windows.
- **Hide the overlay from the overlay.** Unlock it and click the cross next to the lock. Settings > Overlay or the tray menu brings it back.
- **What's new.** After an update the app shows what changed, like this.
- Fixed: the app no longer closes when Windows can't draw the overlay for a moment.

## 1.1.2

- **Stream overlay for OBS.** Settings > Stream overlay (OBS) gives a link for an OBS Browser source, with a Background slider.
- **Warning when a match isn't recorded.** If a match ends without a replay, the app tells you once.
- Copy and paste work in the app.

## 1.1.1

- Damaged settings or match files are restored from a backup instead of being reset.
- Notifications show up as Fortnite Chest Counter.
- Squadmates' numbers arrive on time in any time zone.

## 1.1.0

- **Online mode.** Squadmates who run the app send their exact chest counts, so your squad total and prize odds include them in full.
- The overlay has a lock: unlock it to move and resize the overlay.
