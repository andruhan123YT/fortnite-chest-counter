# Changelog

Short notes per version. The app shows the new ones once after an update, and
Settings > Updates shows them again.

## 1.1.3

- **Leaderboard.** Everyone who runs the app with Online on, by chests, best match and matches, for all time, this week or today. Click a column to sort. Settings > Online shows you as Anonymous instead of your name.
- **Starts with Fortnite.** After you sign in to Windows the app waits quietly and opens in the tray once Fortnite starts. Settings > Windows switches back to starting with Windows.
- **Hide the overlay from the overlay.** Unlock it and click the cross next to the lock. Settings > Overlay or the tray menu brings it back.
- **What's new.** After an update the app shows what changed, like this.
- Fixed: the app no longer closes when Windows can't draw the overlay for a moment.

## 1.1.2

- **Stream overlay for OBS.** Settings > Stream overlay (OBS) gives a link for an OBS Browser source, with a Background slider.
- **Warning when a match isn't recorded.** If a match ends without a replay, the app tells you once.
- Copy and paste work in the app.

## 1.1.1

- Damaged settings or match files are restored from a backup instead of being reset.
- Notifications show up as Fortnite Chest Counter.
- Squadmates' numbers arrive on time in any time zone.

## 1.1.0

- **Online mode.** Squadmates who run the app send their exact chest counts, so your squad total and prize odds include them in full.
- The overlay has a lock: unlock it to move and resize the overlay.
